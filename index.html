Active Research Projects

Diverse research opportunities spanning basic science to translational applications

🧬

Alzheimer's Biomarker Discovery

Using phage-display technology to identify novel peptide biomarkers for early Alzheimer's detection. Students learn molecular cloning, protein expression, and mass spectrometry techniques.

🧪

Tau Protein Research

Investigating post-translational modifications of tau protein and their role in neurodegeneration. Projects include recombinant protein production, phosphorylation studies, and aggregation assays.

🎯

Cancer RTK Research

Using phage-display to target receptor tyrosine kinases (EGFR, HER2) to inhibit cancer cell growth and metastasis. Students learn cell transfection, protein screening, and migration assays.

🕷️

Spider Silk Synthesis

Engineering M13 bacteriophages to produce synthetic spider silk proteins. This biomimetic approach creates high-strength materials for textiles, medical devices, and composites.

🤖

RISE-UP: Robotic Pet Therapy

Investigating how robotic pets can improve sleep and reduce stress for dementia caregivers. Students develop assessment protocols and intervention strategies.

🌐

Dementia Education Website

Creating accessible digital health resources to educate the public about dementia and its subtypes. Students learn health communication and web development.

🔬

Calpain Inhibition Research

Investigating the specificity of pentapeptide inhibitors for calpain proteases. Students conduct enzyme kinetics studies with implications for neurodegenerative disease therapeutics.
