The Guttmann Research Lab has a new home on the web

Lab newsNovember 24, 2026 by Rodney Guttmann

The Guttmann Research Lab website now has its own address: guttmannlab.org. Everything that was on the old address is here, and the old address forwards visitors to the new one.

What you will find on the site

The lab studies Alzheimer's disease and related dementias at the University of West Florida. The main site describes our research projects, including our work on early detection with phage display technology and a study of how robotic pets may help family caregivers of people living with dementia. It also introduces the undergraduate researchers who make the work possible and shows where our alumni go next.

What is new

This news page is new. About once a week we plan to share short, plain language posts in three areas: research explainers that describe what scientists know about Alzheimer's disease and why it matters, practical resources for families and caregivers, and updates on student programs such as the NeuroNauts Scholars Program for high school students and undergraduate research opportunities at UWF.

You can follow along with the news feed in any feed reader, or just check back here.

Join us

Students interested in research are welcome to explore the main site and reach out. Our curriculum vitae is also available as an accessible PDF.
